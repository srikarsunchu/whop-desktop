A guided setup for new downloaders, plus creative work inside the assistant.

- Connect Whop, choose your business, and connect Claude through first-launch setup.
- Use existing logins or explore the fictional demo. Skip Claude to start in Overview; reopen setup from Account anytime.
- Review image briefs in chat, import artwork, edit in Studio, and save local ad drafts with the product, price, and copy attached.
- Restore conversations and creative work, with improvements across the business pages and interactions.

**Install:** Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 11+. Open the DMG and drag Whop Desktop into Applications. The app is signed with Developer ID, notarized by Apple, and stapled; the DMG wrapper is signed.

Whop CLI installation is required for live business data. Chat requires Claude Code installed and signed in. Live image generation uses your Whop balance and requires confirmation; ad drafts do not publish campaigns. Demo Studio uses labeled samples.

Validation: production build, native onboarding walkthrough with existing connections, browser walkthrough of missing-install and skip-Claude paths, and assistant, creative, media, business, and campaign checks. Fresh installation/OAuth on a clean Mac and paid generation were not tested for this release. Imported artwork uses limited local storage; repeated large imports can exhaust it. Campaign lists show the first 100 results; advanced targeting and lead forms remain limited.
